# Product marketing context, Crescens Labs

Written for Phase 7 of `docs/BUILD-PLAN.md`. Every copy decision on the
site defers to this file when it conflicts with instinct.

## The studio

Crescens Labs is a two person end to end software studio, remote from
Indonesia. Founders: Dylansius Putra (PM, fullstack) and Daffa Hasanal
Arkaan (lead engineer). No subcontractors, no account layer.

## The offer

Build the whole system: structure the problem, architect the solution,
iterate it with the client in the room, then train their team and hand
over everything. The client ends able to operate and extend the system
without us. Handover is the definition of the work, not an upsell.

## The audience

Operators of small and mid businesses whose actual problem is unclear
when they arrive: photobooth studios, franchise chains, kost operators,
teams drowning in disconnected apps. They know their business better
than we do. Our job is to find the problem worth solving, then build it
with them.

## Positioning

Most clients arrive sure of the symptom. Judged proof, not self
declared: National Campus Hackathon winner (Solana Foundation with
Colosseum), Top 7 Llama AI Accelerator as the only active student team
through, 1st of 100+ in Grab's marketing competition. Client work:
SimplyBox, RoyaleCard Arena, Snapose (offline first photobooth platform,
5 apps to 1), an Ayam Taliwang franchise system in build.

## Voice

Plain, short, sourced. No em dashes. Every number carries a source,
because the tagline "Don't trust. Verify." is a promise the content
model has to keep. Say the honest thing plainly rather than the
impressive thing vaguely.
