+ PROCESS

Structure meets shipping.

A handover is not a zip file and a goodbye. It is training that runs until your team is faster without us than they were with us.

Five stages. You own the system at the end of stage five, and every stage before it exists to make that transfer real rather than ceremonial.

Most studios optimise for the length of the engagement. We optimise for the day we leave, which is a harder thing to sell and a much easier thing to prove.

MY RECOMMENDATION

A, Instrument, with B's quote spine borrowed in.

Density displays substance

Five systems, two placements, a marketing win, and a genuinely unusual offline-sync story. B spends that one proof point per screen of air. A puts three on screen at once, so a fast scroller still absorbs volume.

Mono blocks are literal here

You sell operating systems for businesses, not brand identity. STATUS / STACK / DURATION is the vocabulary of the actual work. On an agency site that treatment is affectation. On yours it is documentation.

C is the one I would want to build

And I am still not recommending it. Your live engagements are a franchise chain and a kost operator. Zero radius, heavy grain, mono-primary is a harder sell to that buyer than to a crypto audience.

So: A's grid, density, mono blocks, and 2px radius, plus B's scroll-revealed quote per section and B's 192px padding instead of 160px. A is dense and needs a breath. That quote is where the human voice lives, and without it A risks reading as a spec sheet.
